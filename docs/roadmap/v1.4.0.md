# v1.4.0 Delivered Scope

**Theme:** Adaptive Sampling & Telemetry Shaping.

## Delivered

- deterministic sampling by event/config identity
- queue-pressure-aware rate reduction
- per-rule minimum sampling floors
- error/severity/audit/deployment/security protection
- high-latency protection
- incident-tag protection
- tag drop/rename transforms
- safe oversized-payload dropping
- protected tags/payload preserved unless `shape_protected` is explicitly enabled
- full-fidelity Flight Recorder before shaping
- successful sampled-out early-stop semantics
- fail-open behavior when shaping audit persistence is unavailable
- retry-stable compact per-event decision ledger
- exactly-once minute aggregate shaping statistics across retries
- non-destructive shadow shaping
- tenant-scoped shaping APIs/dashboard
- Prometheus sampling decision/pressure metrics
- `telemetryctl shaping validate`
- guarded `telemetryctl shaping prune` lifecycle management
- frozen-incident `telemetryctl shaping preview`
- explicit event/byte/protected/source-type retention measurements
- `make demo-shaping`
- ADRs 0033-0035

## Deliberately deferred

v1.4 does not yet implement:

- distributed/cluster-global pressure control;
- trace-tail sampling requiring complete trace assembly;
- autonomous policy promotion;
- arbitrary JSON-path payload transforms;
- destructive ingestion-time PII redaction;
- vendor-specific sampling cost formulas.

Those would require broader lifecycle/connector capabilities planned for later
1.x releases.
