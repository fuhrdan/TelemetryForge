# Code of Conduct

TelemetryForge welcomes technical discussion, bug reports, design criticism,
and contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Project participation should remain professional and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person presenting them.
- Give enough technical context for another contributor to reproduce or review
  a claim.
- Treat questions asked in good faith as part of collaboration, not as a test of
  status or seniority.
- Respect privacy and do not post credentials, private telemetry, customer data,
  or another person's identifying information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to propose an
  alternative with supporting evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private
information, sexual harassment, or sustained disruption of project spaces is
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these rules
and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
Security-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than
being posted publicly.
